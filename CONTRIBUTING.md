# Contributing to Organic Intelligence

## Organize before expanding

Add ideas to the appropriate document instead of appending everything to the README:

- definition, principles, or Natural Learning → `docs/01-foundations.md`
- relationship between OI and AI → `docs/02-oi-and-ai.md`
- process, architecture, or concept template → `docs/03-operating-model.md`
- real-world domain → `docs/04-applications.md`
- evidence, experiments, metrics, or ethics → `docs/05-research-roadmap.md`
- terminology → `docs/06-glossary.md`

## Bilingual writing pattern

Write the English explanation first and place the Telugu explanation directly after it. Keep the meaning aligned rather than translating word-for-word. If a translation is uncertain, mark the term and explain the uncertainty.

## Quality checklist

- Is the concept clearly defined in one sentence?
- Is it grounded in an observation, a design problem, or both?
- Does it distinguish evidence from speculation?
- Does it describe benefits and risks?
- Does it preserve human agency and ecological responsibility?
- Is the Telugu version understandable and aligned with the English version?

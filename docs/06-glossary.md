# 6. English–Telugu Glossary

| English | తెలుగు | Working meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Organic Intelligence | ఆర్గానిక్ ఇంటెలిజెన్స్ / సజీవ మేధస్సు | Intelligence expressed by living systems and used as a human-centred design lens. |
| Natural Learning | సహజ అభ్యాసం | Learning through continuous interaction with an environment. |
| Embodiment | శరీర ఆధారిత అనుభవం | The way a body’s needs and limits shape intelligence. |
| Context | సందర్భం | The history, relationships, place, and conditions that give an event meaning. |
| Self-awareness | స్వీయ అవగాహన | The ability to reflect on one’s own state or activity. |
| Empathy | సానుభూతి | Recognizing and responding to another being’s experience. |
| Moral reasoning | నైతిక తర్కం | Reasoning about what ought to be done and who may be affected. |
| Adaptation | అనుకూలత | Changing behaviour or structure in response to conditions. |
| Resilience | స్థైర్యం / పునరుద్ధరణ సామర్థ్యం | The ability to absorb disruption and continue or recover. |
| Self-organization | స్వయం-వ్యవస్థీకరణ | Order emerging from local interactions without one central controller. |
| Feedback loop | ఫీడ్‌బ్యాక్ చక్రం | A process where results influence the next action. |
| Emergence | ఉద్భవ లక్షణం | A system-level pattern arising from many local interactions. |
| Biomimicry | జీవానుకరణ | Learning design ideas from biological forms and processes. |
| Human agency | మానవ స్వాతంత్ర్యం | People’s ability to understand, influence, refuse, and change a decision. |
| Audit trail | నిర్ణయ చరిత్ర / ఆడిట్ ట్రయిల్ | A transparent record of inputs, reasoning, changes, and outcomes. |
| Ecological balance | పర్యావరణ సమతుల్యత | Considering effects across the wider living system. |
| Responsible scale | బాధ్యతాయుత విస్తరణ | Growing a system only when governance, safety, and maintenance are ready. |
| Grace | అనుగ్రహం | A lived or spiritual sense of life-giving possibility beyond personal control; a provisional OI concept, not a measured technical property. |
| Karma / Karman | కర్మ | Intentional action and its moral consequences across relationships, living systems, and—within rebirth-oriented traditions—future lives; a provisional OI concept, not a measured AI property. |
| Samsara | సంసారం | The cycle of birth, death, and rebirth in traditions that use this concept. |
| Rebirth | పునర్జన్మ | A philosophical or religious account of continuity beyond one bodily life; not presented here as scientifically verified. |
| Ripple effect | ప్రభావ తరంగం | The direct and indirect consequences an action creates across a person, relationships, society, ecology, and future generations. |

## Translation note

Telugu terms may evolve as the community develops better language. Where a technical English term is more precise, keep the English term in parentheses and explain it in plain Telugu.

తెలుగు పదాలు సమాజం అభివృద్ధి చెందుతున్న కొద్దీ మెరుగుపడవచ్చు. సాంకేతిక ఆంగ్ల పదం మరింత ఖచ్చితంగా ఉన్నప్పుడు, దాన్ని బంధనాల్లో ఉంచి సాధారణ తెలుగులో అర్థం వివరించాలి.

## What an approved licence should specify

- exact files, passages, or concepts covered;
- permitted medium, audience, geography, and duration;
- whether modification, translation, or derivative work is allowed;
- required author credit and links;
- royalty or fee, payment dates, reporting, and audit rights;
- whether sublicensing is prohibited;
- termination conditions and treatment of existing copies.

An email conversation is not a complete licence unless it clearly states the permission scope and is accepted in writing by the rights holder.
